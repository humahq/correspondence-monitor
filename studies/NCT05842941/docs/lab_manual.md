# 5. Specimen Processing & Blood Volumes

Strict adherence to the Order of Draw (CLSI H3-A6) is required. Blood must be collected in the sequence: Light Blue → Gold SST → Lavender EDTA. Always program centrifuges using Relative Centrifugal Force (RCF or g), not RPM.

Table columns: Tube Type / Color | Target Assay | Processing Instructions
- Tube Type / Color: Light Blue (Sodium Citrate) | Target Assay: Coagulation | Processing Instructions: Invert 3-4x. Centrifuge at 1,500 x g for 15 min at Room Temp (20-25°C). Transfer plasma to cryovial. Freeze immediately.
- Tube Type / Color: Gold SST (Serum Separator) | Target Assay: Safety Chem, Endocrinology | Processing Instructions: Invert 5x. Allow to clot upright for 30 min. Centrifuge at 1,500 - 2,000 x g for 10-15 min at Room Temp. Ship ambient.
- Tube Type / Color: Lavender EDTA (3.0 mL) | Target Assay: Safety Hematology | Processing Instructions: Invert 8-10x. Do Not Centrifuge. Ship as ambient whole blood on day of collection.
- Tube Type / Color: Lavender EDTA (4.0 mL / 6.0 mL) | Target Assay: PK Plasma & Biomarkers | Processing Instructions: Invert 8-10x. Centrifuge at 1,500 - 2,000 x g for 10-15 min at 4°C (Refrigerated). Separate plasma into cryovials. Freeze at -70°C within 60 mins.
- Tube Type / Color: Specialty Tubes | Target Assay: Genomics / DNA | Processing Instructions: Invert 8-10x. Do Not Centrifuge. Freeze immediately at -70°C.

# 6. Packing and Shipping Instructions

All specimens must be transported in accordance with national regulations and IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations for UN 3373 Biological Substance, Category B. Use the specific supplies provided by Labcorp/World Courier.

## Step 1: Place samples into Diagnostic Specimens Bag (STP 741)

- Ensure all primary tubes and cryovials are tightly sealed.
- Place samples into the clear plastic STP 741 bag.
- Insert the absorbent material strip (STP 152) into the bag alongside the tubes.
- Remove excess air and seal the bag using the adhesive strip.

## Step 2: Place Bag into Secondary Packaging

- Place the sealed plastic STP 741 bag inside the white Tyvek/paper STP 740 bag (if provided) or directly into the insulated thermal box.

## Step 3: Temperature Control (Thermal Box & Dry Ice)

- For Ambient Shipments: Place the sealed bag into the standard ambient cardboard box.
- For Frozen Shipments: Place the sealed bag into the EPS foam insulated thermal box (M9.5). The World Courier driver will pour the appropriate amount of Dry Ice (minimum 5.0 kg to 17.0 kg depending on transit time) over the samples.

## Mandatory Documentation

Each shipment tendered to the courier must be accompanied by the following documentation:

- House Air Waybill (HAWB): Affix to the exterior of the shipment. This is your internal tracking form provided by World Courier.
- Sample Requisition Form (PRF/GSR): Place the completed Requisition Form inside the shipping container, but outside the sealed biohazard bag. Retain a photocopy for the subject's chart.
- Customs Invoice (International Only): Print the Customs Invoice (Appendix 3) on your hospital/institution letterhead and sign it. Required for cross-border shipments to declare "No Commercial Value".
- USDA Statement (International to USA): Required for animal/human material transit to the US. See Appendix 3.
