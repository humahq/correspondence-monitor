# MAP Laboratory Manual v1.0

PLACEHOLDER DOCUMENT. This laboratory manual was written for the correspondence monitor demo because no lab manual for NCT04098666 is publicly available. It is modelled on common central-laboratory practice for plasma biomarker studies. It is not a sponsor document and must be replaced with the sponsor's own lab manual before any real use.

## 1. Scope

This manual describes how blood samples for the Metformin in Alzheimer's dementia Prevention (MAP) study are collected, processed, labeled, stored and shipped. It applies to all sites. Samples collected for the study are the screening safety laboratory panel, hemoglobin A1c, and plasma for Alzheimer's disease biomarkers at Screening/Baseline, Month 12 and Month 18.

## 2. Kits and supplies

Each visit uses one visit-specific kit supplied by the central laboratory. Do not use a kit after the expiry date printed on the kit box. Do not mix tubes between kits. Sites must keep at least two unexpired kits on hand for each upcoming visit type.

## 3. Participant preparation

Participants must fast for at least 8 hours before the blood draw (water is allowed). If the participant has not fasted, record the time of their last meal on the requisition form; do not cancel the draw.

## 4. Collection

Plasma for biomarkers is collected in one 10 mL K2EDTA (lavender top) tube. Immediately after collection, gently invert the tube 8 to 10 times. Do not shake the tube. Record the exact time of the draw on the requisition form. The biomarker tube must be drawn after the serum tube to avoid additive carry-over.

## 5. Processing of plasma

The K2EDTA tube must be centrifuged within 60 minutes of the blood draw. Centrifuge at 2000 x g for 10 minutes at 4 degrees C. After centrifugation, transfer the plasma into 0.5 mL aliquots in the supplied polypropylene cryovials. Do not use polystyrene tubes. Aliquots must be placed in a freezer at -80 degrees C within 2 hours of the blood draw. Samples that were not frozen within 2 hours of the blood draw must still be shipped but must be marked as a processing deviation on the requisition form, and the central laboratory must be told in the shipment notification.

## 6. Labeling

Every tube and cryovial must carry a printed kit label showing the participant ID, visit, collection date and collection time. Handwritten labels are not acceptable. Unlabeled or mislabeled samples will be rejected by the central laboratory.

## 7. Storage

Frozen aliquots must be stored at -80 degrees C until shipment. Storage at -20 degrees C is permitted for no more than 24 hours if a -80 degrees C freezer is temporarily unavailable. Samples must not be thawed and refrozen. Each freezer used for MAP samples must have a continuous temperature log.

## 8. Shipping

Frozen samples are shipped on dry ice to the central laboratory. Shipments may only be sent Monday through Wednesday, and never on the day before a public holiday, so that they do not arrive on a weekend or holiday. The site must email a shipment notification to the central laboratory at least 24 hours before each shipment. Each shipment must include the completed requisition forms and a copy of the freezer temperature log covering the storage period.

## 9. Hemolysis and recollection

Hemolyzed samples are not acceptable for biomarker analysis. If a sample is visibly hemolyzed, a recollection must be scheduled within the visit window.
